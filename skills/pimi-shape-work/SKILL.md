---
name: pimi-shape-work
description: Turn a broad or fuzzy product request into one useful change before editing.
---

- Say what the person should be able to do, in their words. Name where they start and what they should see at the end.
- Check how that flow works today. Separate the requested behavior from ideas you can try later.
- Make small, reversible choices yourself. Ask one short question only when the answer would change the work or risk losing something.
- Pick the smallest change someone can use all the way through. Keep related work in separate commits when it can ship on its own.
