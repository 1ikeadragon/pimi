---
name: pimi-walk-the-flow
description: Verify a user-facing fix or release by walking the real app flow with realistic data.
---

- Start where a person would. Click or type through the shipped UI with a real input or a disposable project.
- Check the thing that should actually change. Read the saved file, reopen the chat, or inspect the board. A success toast is only a clue.
- Try the likeliest interruption, such as switching views mid-save or opening the same link twice.
- Say which steps you ran and where you used fixture data. Fix gaps before calling the flow ready.
